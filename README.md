## 🌷 About Us

**Little Moments Studio** creates simple, personalized and responsive websites for life's special occasions.

We focus on clean designs, thoughtful messages and little interactive touches that make a digital surprise feel personal. 💕

* 🎂 Birthday surprise websites
* 💌 Anniversary greeting websites
* 🫶 Friendship and appreciation pages
